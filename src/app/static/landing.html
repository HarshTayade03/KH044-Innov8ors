PLATFORM CAPABILITIES

One workspace for the work between detection and remediation.

Designed around the decisions officers actually have to defend.

IN

Scanner intake and provenance

Accept JSON and SARIF exports from Burp Suite, Nessus, OWASP ZAP, Snyk, Trivy, or a manual analyst record. Each normalized finding retains scanner, batch, source file, quality, and evidence context.

Upload · normalize · retain source
AI

Readable vulnerability intelligence

Transform raw records into description, location, reproduction, and impact views. Redaction runs before derived content is displayed or embedded, making the output useful for investigation and controlled sharing.

Four views · redaction · embeddings
01

Conservative duplicate detection

Fingerprint groups and optional semantic clustering reduce repeat work while hard compatibility gates protect findings that differ by host, asset, package, or parameter. Every cluster exposes its merge basis.

Fingerprint · guarded similarity · split/merge history
R

Explainable risk prioritization

Scores combine severity, exploit probability, exposure, asset context, and validation state. Analysts can inspect each contribution instead of receiving an opaque number with no operational meaning.

Contributions · tiers · remediation context
EV

Controlled validation evidence

Request a clearly labeled offline SQL injection, XSS, or SSRF lab scenario. Redacted artifacts are hash-checked on retrieval, and the interface makes clear that simulation is not proof of exploitability.

Allowlist · artifacts · integrity checks
AU

Cases, review, and accountability

Assemble canonical issues into cases, request more evidence, approve or reject with a reason, override priority, and inspect the cross-entity audit stream. Human approval remains the control point.

Case queue · review history · immutable audit log
